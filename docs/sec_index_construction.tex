\subsection{Index Construction and Throughput}
\label{sec:indexConstruction}
We build one index per system for each dataset (DEEP, TTI and MSMARCO, with 10M
and 100M vectors) and each cluster size (4, 6 and 8 nodes), with the
parameters of the original papers: GP-ANN$^{*}$ partitions the data into as
many shards as nodes with overlap 0.2, Pyramid$^{*}$ builds a meta-HNSW over
10K centroids and one sub-HNSW per node, CoTra builds one partition per node,
DistributedANN$^{*}$ uses 32 (10M) and 256 (100M) clusters, and Harmony builds a
single IVF index with 4096 (10M) and 10000 (100M) lists, which is the same for
every cluster size. Table~\ref{tab:memory_indexing} reports the peak memory per
node of each construction, and Figure~\ref{fig:build_time} its time.

\textbf{Memory.} On 10M and 100M vectors of DEEP and TTI every system fits in
our nodes, but on MSMARCO100M (286\,GiB of 768-dimensional vectors) only CoTra
and DistributedANN$^{*}$ complete the construction. GP-ANN$^{*}$, Pyramid$^{*}$
and Harmony run out of memory even on our largest nodes (493\,GiB), because a
phase of their construction runs on a single node and needs a second copy of the
vectors besides the dataset itself (for the routing index of GP-ANN$^{*}$, the
inner-product transformation of Pyramid$^{*}$ and the IVF lists of Harmony),
which exceeds the memory of a node. For Pyramid$^{*}$ on MSMARCO, the graph of centroids given to the KaHIP
partitioner is made undirected first, since KaHIP requires it and the directed
HNSW graph makes it fail.

\textbf{Time.} Harmony is by far the fastest to build, from 1~minute (DEEP10M) to
23~minutes (TTI100M), since an IVF index only requires training the centroids and
assigning the vectors to them. On 10M vectors of DEEP and TTI, GP-ANN$^{*}$, CoTra
and DistributedANN$^{*}$ take between 10 and 40~minutes, while on 100M vectors
they take 2--4~hours (GP-ANN$^{*}$, CoTra) and 3--8~hours
(DistributedANN$^{*}$). The higher dimensionality of MSMARCO makes construction
3--7$\times$ slower than on TTI with the same number of vectors for GP-ANN$^{*}$,
CoTra and DistributedANN$^{*}$, and
MSMARCO100M takes 14--19~hours with CoTra and 17--33~hours with
DistributedANN$^{*}$. Pyramid$^{*}$ shows the widest variation: a few minutes on
TTI10M, but hours on DEEP10M and about two days on DEEP100M, where almost all
the time is spent in the hierarchical balanced k-means of the original code.

\textbf{Scalability.} Adding nodes shortens the construction only for the systems
whose construction is fully distributed: going from 4 to 8~nodes reduces the
time of CoTra and DistributedANN$^{*}$ by 24--55\% across all datasets. For the
other systems, the dominant phase runs on a single node and does not benefit from
more nodes. Harmony builds the same index regardless of the cluster size.
GP-ANN$^{*}$ spends 80--95\% of its time in the single-node partitioning and
QueryAttribution (routing index and routing tables), so only the construction of
the shards is distributed and its total time stays almost constant (on TTI10M it
even grows from 35 to 40~minutes). For
Pyramid$^{*}$, the k-means and the partitioning of the centroids run on a single
node and dominate the total, so the construction time does not decrease
consistently with the number of nodes; it does so only on MSMARCO10M, where it
drops from 1.8 to 1.0~hours.
