\caption{Recall@10 vs.\ throughput on 4, 6 and 8 nodes (16~threads per node, $k{=}10$;
10{,}000 queries on DEEP and TTI, 9{,}376 on MSMARCO; QPS on a logarithmic scale). Each
curve is the Pareto front of a system, i.e.\ its fastest configuration for each recall
level; dashed vertical lines mark the recall targets (0.80, 0.90, 0.95, 0.99; also 0.70 on
TTI10M; 0.80, 0.90, 0.92 on TTI100M with 8 nodes, d.3). On TTI10M (c) DistributedANN$^*$
reaches at most 0.76--0.77 and Harmony stays below 0.70 (0.40--0.56), so it does not appear;
on TTI100M (d) both stay below 0.60 and are not shown. DistributedANN$^*$ and Harmony are
not evaluated on MSMARCO (e, f); on MSMARCO100M (f) only CoTra builds its index
(Table~\ref{tab:memory_indexing}). DistributedANN$^*$ saturates at recall 0.93 on DEEP100M (b),
CoTra at 0.97 on MSMARCO, and GP-ANN$^*$ at 0.979 and 0.982 on TTI10M with 4 and 6 nodes
(c.1, c.2). The same operating points are used for the load and communication analysis
(Section~\ref{sec:load-comm}).}
